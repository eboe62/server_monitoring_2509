# Skill Lifecycle Management

## Purpose

This document defines how skills are created, reviewed, superseded, and retired.

---

## Lifecycle States

PROPOSED

ACTIVE

DEPRECATED

SUPERSEDED

ARCHIVED

---

## Creation Process

Requirements:

* documented purpose
* owner
* version
* governance alignment
* registry entry

Until completed:

Status = PROPOSED

---

## Activation Process

Requirements:

* governance review
* registry entry
* metadata completed

Result:

Status = ACTIVE

---

## Review Process

Triggers:

* ADR change
* governance change
* annual review
* incident review

Review must evaluate:

* relevance
* correctness
* governance compliance

---

## Deprecation Process

Triggers:

* replacement available
* governance change
* obsolete functionality

Status:

DEPRECATED

---

## Supersession Process

Requirements:

* replacement identified
* registry updated

Status:

SUPERSEDED

---

## Archival Process

Requirements:

* no operational value
* retained for history

Status:

ARCHIVED

---

## Governance Rule

Skill changes are incomplete until:

SKILL_REGISTRY.md

has been updated.

---

## Final Principle

Skills are governed assets.

Not disposable prompts.
